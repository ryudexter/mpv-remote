# MPV Player Remote — Privacy Policy

*Effective October 17, 2026*

MPV Player Remote does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What the app does with information

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or
  tracking code, and does not use any third-party services.
- **Local network only.** The app talks directly to the MPV Player Remote
  companion on your own Mac, over your Wi-Fi network. Nothing is sent to us or
  to anyone else over the internet.
- **What stays on your devices.** To reconnect without asking again, your
  iPhone remembers the Macs you paired with and a random pairing token for
  each, and the companion on your Mac keeps the matching token along with your
  phone's name. Both stay on those devices. Remove the iPhone's copy by
  deleting the app; the Mac's copy is in
  `~/Library/Application Support/MPV Remote/paired-devices.json`.
- **Playback information.** What mpv is playing (titles, tracks, playlist,
  and a small still of the frame on screen) travels only between your Mac and
  your iPhone, to show it in the app. The still isn't kept anywhere.

## Local network permission

iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. The app uses
it only to discover and connect to the companion on your Mac.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
effective date.
